Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Who We Are

Nexus Media Network ("we," "us," "our") operates a portfolio of branded short-form video channels. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with our content publishing platform and website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you contact us, we collect your name and email address.
  • YouTube API Data: Our publishing platform uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to channels we own and manage. Through Google OAuth, we access the youtube.upload scope, which allows uploading videos to authorized YouTube channels. We do not access, read, or store your personal YouTube watch history, subscriptions, or private playlists.
  • Usage Data: We collect aggregated analytics on video performance (views, engagement) for channels we operate. This data is used solely for editorial decisions.

3. How We Use YouTube API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We use YouTube API Services solely to upload video content to YouTube channels owned and operated by Nexus Media Network.
  • We do not transfer YouTube API data to third parties.
  • We do not use YouTube API data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not use YouTube API data to train machine learning models.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption (Fernet/AES). Access tokens are never logged in plaintext. We retain API credentials only for as long as needed to operate our publishing platform.

5. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing contact@nexusmedia.network with the subject "Data Deletion Request." We will process verified requests within 30 days. Revoking our app's access via your Google Account permissions page immediately stops all API access; stored tokens are purged within 30 days of revocation.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Google's YouTube Data API v3 subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be noted with a revised "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@nexusmedia.network
